Rulebook FAQ

How do I find a specific rule?

Use the search box or press / anywhere on the page. Search checks rule titles and full rule text.

Can I link someone directly to a rule?

Yes. Open a rule and use the copy-link button. The URL will jump directly to that section.

What if I am unsure how a rule applies?

Use the Discord button and follow NHRP's ticket/report process. The rulebook itself remains the source text for this site.
